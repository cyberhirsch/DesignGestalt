Classroom tools

Gestalt Playground

Push each principle until the grouping flips. Proximity, similarity, closure, continuity and figure-ground as live demonstrations.

Contrast Checker

Style Lottery

Assigns each student one design style to present, drawn from 62 movements — Arts & Crafts through the diffusion-model look. Two redraws each.

Type Lottery

Practice

The Bézier Game

Pen-tool drills: trace each shape using as few anchor points as possible. Worth an hour before you trace anything in Illustrator.

Week 6 · External · Method of Action

Color

Matching drills for hue and saturation, then complementary, analogous, triadic and tetradic relationships. Trains the eye the theory assumes you already have.

Week 7 · External · María Munuera & Mark MacKay

KernType

A letter-spacing game — nudge each pair until the rhythm between the letters is even, then compare against a typographer's answer. Scored out of 100.

Week 10 · External · Method of Action

Shape Type

Pull letterforms back into shape and score how close you got. The fastest way to start noticing what a curve on an a is actually doing.
