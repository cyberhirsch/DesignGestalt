Methods and tests

Design thinking has six modes, and each has its own methods: ways to look, to understand, to decide, to make ideas, to build and to check. Every brief names its mode. Here are the methods for each, with the week that teaches them.

Why test at all? With Gestalt principles you can argue for or against almost any design: the same closure can be called clever or confusing.

The principles explain why something works or fails. A test, with people who haven't seen your work, decides whether it does.

Methods are added here as their week is taught. Crazy 8s comes from Google Ventures' design sprints; the five-user rule from Jakob Nielsen (2000); SCAMPER from Bob Eberle (1971), after Alex Osborn's checklist.
